The rules this follows (for you to approve)
  1. The same header on every quiz. ◂ Back (to where you came from) and ⌂ Island on the left; the subject and quiz title in the middle; ⓘ Rules and ⏱ time limit on the right; a one-line "how it works" underneath.
  2. Subject colour. The header uses the subject's colour: a pale tint with deep-coloured text in light mode, and the reverse in dark mode. Selected tabs and options use it too.
  3. Title Case for every title (Countries of the World, Who's Who, Symbols and Sacred Animals). Small words stay lower case: a, an, the, and, of, in, on, to, with, for.
  4. Topics (if a quiz has more than one) are a row of equal-width tabs, with one line about the chosen topic underneath.
  5. Every playable thing is a row: number or ★, name and description, Easy / Medium / Hard, then Start. "Learn it first" or "Study the board" is always the first row, with a dashed border.
  6. What Easy, Medium and Hard mean: Easy = Learn it first and the board shown; Medium = hints available; Hard = no hints. A quiz can set its own details (e.g. Hard Countries adds territories). Levels not built yet show as dashed and can't be picked.
  7. Special challenges are two cards side by side; extras ("More cultures") are centred chips at the bottom.
  8. Type or click is a clear switch near the top, only on quizzes that have both.
  9. Symmetry. Equal-width buttons, centred titles and groups, and even rows (two or four across, never a lonely odd one where it can be helped).
  10. Ticks. A ✓ on each level you've done, and a green ✓ badge on a row when it's finished. These feed the passport.
